Good morning, my love.

Before the world gets loud, before the to-do lists, before anything else — I need you to know that you were the very first thought I had when I opened my eyes today.

Not in a grand, fireworks kind of way. Just quietly, the way sunlight finds its way into a room: certain, warm, and a little bit miraculous.

I love the way you laugh before the joke is even finished. I love that you talk to cats on the street like they owe you an explanation. I love that you are somehow the softest and the strongest person I have ever met.

Today has absolutely nothing planned for you except to be wonderful. Stretch, breathe, drink your coffee while it is actually hot — and know that somewhere nearby, someone is stupidly, helplessly, completely in love with you.

And on the harder days — the ones where everything feels heavy and nothing sits right — I want you to save this page and come back to it.

Because none of that changes this: you are not too much. You are not behind. You are not alone. You are exactly where you are supposed to be, and I am right here beside you, in every version of every day.

You do not have to earn rest. You do not have to be perfect to be loved. You already are, entirely, just as you are — messy and brilliant and irreplaceable.

I choose you on the easy mornings and on the hard ones. Always.

— yours, entirely  ❤
